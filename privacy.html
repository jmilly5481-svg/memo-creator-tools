Privacy Policy

Memo Creator Tools is operated by Memo Labs. Last updated: September 22, 2026.

What we collect

When you connect a TikTok account we receive an access token, a refresh token and your TikTok open ID (scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload). We do not receive your password. We store the videos you choose to upload until they have been delivered to TikTok.

How we use it

Tokens are used only to upload the videos you choose to your own TikTok inbox and to check upload status. We do not read your messages, followers, analytics or other account data, and we do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Where it is stored

Tokens and videos are stored on the operator's own computer, not on a shared server, and are not transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's API.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until you disconnect the app. You can revoke access at any time from TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); we then delete the stored token. You can also email us to request deletion of any stored data.
